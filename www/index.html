Questions, answered.

Is it free?

Yes. There are no accounts, subscriptions, or ads.

Does it work offline?

Yes. After your first visit the website works offline, and the Android app works offline from the start.

Where is my data stored?

Settings, favorites, and events are saved on your own device and never sent to a server. See the privacy policy.

How accurate are the Nepali BS dates?

BS dates are built in for 2082–2088 BS. Dates for 2083 match published calendars; later years come from public converter tables, so check important dates against an official patro before printing.

How do I install the Android app?

Tap Download for Android, open the file, and allow the install if Android asks. The file is the Kalendar app installer.

Can I use my own photos?

Yes. Add photos in the app to try them on a design, and the project owner can bundle final photos so they load automatically.
